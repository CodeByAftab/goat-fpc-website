About Us

Empowering farmers, enriching lives through sustainable goat farming and collective growth.

Organization Profile

Goat Agri Farmer Producer Company Limited (Goat Agri FPC) is a registered Farmer Producer Company operating in Raipur and nearby blocks of Chhattisgarh. We unite small and marginal farmers to enhance productivity, ensure better market access, and improve livelihoods through sustainable goat farming and value-added services.

Our Vision

To be a leading Farmer Producer Company that transforms goat farming into a sustainable, profitable and dignified livelihood for every farmer in our network.

Our Mission

  • ● Promote integrated and sustainable goat farming practices.
  • ● Provide quality breed, veterinary care and technical support.
  • ● Strengthen farmer capacity and knowledge through training and handholding.
  • ● Ensure better market linkages and value addition for higher income.
  • ● Leverage technology and innovation for efficient and transparent operations.
